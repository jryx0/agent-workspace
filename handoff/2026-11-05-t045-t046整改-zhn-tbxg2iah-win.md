## 2. fix：`worker/src/modules/collect.js`（e988584，+4/-1）

```
-  'SELECT failed_json FROM device_status_log WHERE collected_at = ? AND failed_json IS NOT NULL LIMIT 1',
+  'SELECT MAX(failed_json) AS failed_json FROM device_status_log WHERE collected_at = ? AND failed_json IS NOT NULL',
```

附三行注释：同刻每设备一行可能各带不同 failed_json，MAX() 取确定值（与 runsSummary 的 runs 汇总一致）；聚合无匹配仍返回一行 NULL，`|| {}` 与 `parseFailedJson` 空值保护不变。
